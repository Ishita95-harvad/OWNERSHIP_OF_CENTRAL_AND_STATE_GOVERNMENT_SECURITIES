# 🏦 Ownership of Central and State Government Securities

Welcome to **Ownership of Central and State Government Securities**! This dataset provides insights into the ownership distribution of government securities across various institutional categories, helping researchers analyze trends in public debt, financial markets, and investor participation.

## 📌 Dataset Overview
- 📊 **Government Bond Holdings:** Breakdown of ownership between institutions, private investors, and foreign entities  
- 🏛 **Central vs. State Securities:** Comparison of debt instruments issued by federal and state governments  
- 💰 **Market Trends:** Analysis of interest rate impacts, policy shifts, and financial sector responses  
- 🔍 **Investor Categories:** Understanding how banks, pension funds, insurance companies, and individuals contribute to bond markets  

## 📂 Data Format
The dataset is structured in CSV format with key attributes:
- `Year` – Data collection year  
- `Security Type` – Central or state government-issued bonds  
- `Total Outstanding (INR Crores)` – Total value of securities in circulation  
- `Institutional Ownership (%)` – Percentage of securities held by financial institutions  
- `Foreign Investment (%)` – Share of securities owned by international investors  
- `Retail Investor Share (%)` – Ownership percentage attributed to individual investors
